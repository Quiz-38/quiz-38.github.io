The Scoop: Flash Tetris

Pop Flash Tetris open on a break and it's already loaded before you've settled in. That's kind of the whole point.

Why We Love It

Flash Tetris is a familiar format done well — easy to explain to a friend in one sentence, and still fun after way more sessions than you'd expect.

Quick Tip

If a round of Flash Tetris starts going badly, just restart. Fighting through a bad position usually costs more time than a fresh start would.
