The Scoop: Mr Bullet

Mr Bullet is one of those pick-up-and-go games. Click, wait a beat, you're in.

Why We Love It

Mr Bullet rewards good positioning more than fast trigger fingers. It gets busier as you go, so staying alive is about reading the room early.

Quick Tip

Pay more attention to what ends your runs in Mr Bullet than what keeps them going — that's usually where the easy improvement is hiding.
