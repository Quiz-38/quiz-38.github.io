About

Sunset Arcade is a browser game hub built around one simple idea: click a tile, be playing within a couple seconds. No accounts, nothing to install — every game runs right inside the page.

The library mixes sports, racing, puzzle, arcade, clicker, and multiplayer games, with new titles added over time. Got a suggestion? The Contact page is the place for it.

Sunset Arcade doesn't make the games listed here. Each one belongs to its original creator or publisher — this site just makes them easy to reach from a browser.

The write-ups on each game page are written in-house by the Sunset Arcade team, not supplied by the publishers.
